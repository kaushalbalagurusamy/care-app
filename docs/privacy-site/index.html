Privacy Policy

CARE helps you reflect on your relationships through assessments, education, and exercises. This page explains what the iOS app saves, when an outside service may receive information, and how to remove your data.

Effective October 5, 2026 · Operated by Amy Banks, MD.

What CARE saves on your device

The current iOS app stores its records locally on your device. It does not create a CARE account or upload your profile, contacts, assessment answers, or exercise answers to a CARE server. The app saves:

  • Your profile name, optional profile photo, and assessment reminder preference.
  • People you add, including their names, optional relationship descriptions and photos, and your selection of assessment participants.
  • Assessment progress, answers, scores, and past results, including names associated with those results.
  • Unfinished exercise and quiz progress. After you complete an exercise, CARE keeps completion dates, counts, favorites, and ratings but does not keep your written exercise answers. It also saves education and quiz progress, such as completion dates, attempt counts, and scores.

CARE does not currently sync these records between devices through a CARE service or iCloud. Your iPhone’s own backup settings may separately include app data in an Apple-managed backup.

Photos and videos you choose

Choosing media is optional. CARE uses Apple’s photo picker so you can select specific items. If you add a profile or relationship photo, the app makes a reduced-size copy and saves it locally with that record.

For an unfinished exercise, CARE may save a reference to selected media so you can continue later. A selected video may also be copied temporarily on your device for playback. The original stays in your photo library, which you manage through iOS.

When you use outside services

  • YouTube: Playing an embedded video loads Google’s YouTube player. Google may receive device, network, and playback information under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Messages: If you choose to share an exercise, CARE opens the iOS message composer with content you selected. You decide whether to send it; your message is then handled by your messaging provider.
  • External links: Opening a link to another website takes you to that provider, whose practices may differ from CARE’s.
  • App support: If you submit the CARE support form, the name, reply email, topic, and message you provide are sent through Vercel and Amy Banks, MD’s website to the CARE support team. This information is used to respond to and manage your request. Do not include assessment answers or other sensitive personal information.

This privacy page and the support form are hosted by Vercel. As with other websites, its hosting provider may process routine connection information needed to deliver the pages. The privacy page does not ask you to submit your CARE app data.

Your choices and deletion

You can choose whether to select media, share a message, or allow notifications and Face ID. Manage device permissions in iOS Settings. CARE keeps locally saved records until you remove them in the app or remove the app, subject to any separate device backup you control.

In Profile → Privacy, you can clear profile information, saved relationships, assessment data, or all CARE data. Clearing relationships alone does not remove names already included in past assessment results. To remove those names from this device, also clear assessment data or choose Clear All Data.

Because CARE has no app account or server copy of these records, Amy Banks, MD cannot remotely view or delete the information stored in your app. Use the in-app controls on each device, and manage any iOS backups separately.

Updates to this policy

If CARE’s data practices change, this page will be updated and its effective date revised. The app’s privacy information and App Store disclosures should be updated to match.

Contact

For privacy questions, app problems, feedback, or feature requests, use the RCT CARE support form. Please do not include assessment answers, relationship names, health information, or other sensitive personal information.
