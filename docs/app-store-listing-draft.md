## Proposed description

RCT CARE offers a private place to reflect on your relationships through a C.A.R.E. assessment, short educational lessons, and guided exercises.

Choose five people for an assessment and explore four areas: Calm, Accepted, Resonant, and Energetic. View your results and past assessments to see how your responses change over time. Learn about relationship patterns through education topics and quizzes, then try two free exercises in each C.A.R.E. area. Track completed exercises and days when you practiced.

Your profile, relationships, assessments, and progress are saved on your device. CARE does not require an account or sync these records to a CARE server. Some exercises offer optional embedded YouTube videos; playing one connects to Google. You can complete those exercises without watching a video.

CARE is for education and personal reflection. It does not provide a medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency support.
