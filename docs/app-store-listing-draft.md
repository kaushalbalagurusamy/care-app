## Proposed description

RCT CARE gives you tools to use your brain to improve your relationships with people in your life. Start with a C.A.R.E. assessment across five relationships and explore four areas: Calm, Accepted, Resonant, and Energetic.

See your results, compare assessments over time, and learn about relationship patterns through short lessons and quizzes. Use guided brain based exercises to practice new ways of connecting. This release includes two exercises in each C.A.R.E. area.

Your profile, relationships, assessments, and progress are saved on your device. No CARE account is required. Some exercises offer optional embedded YouTube videos; playing one connects to Google. You can complete those exercises without watching a video.

CARE is for education and personal reflection. It does not provide a medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency support.
